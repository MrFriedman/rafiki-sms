How it works

1. Text one number.

Feature phone or smartphone, field or town.

2. Ask in your own words.

Pests, planting, weather. No forms, no menu.

3. Get a short reply, then a follow-up.

Rafiki remembers, so “and if I cannot buy pesticide?” still makes sense. Plain SMS, short enough to read once.

Built for farmers first

Farming

Pests, planting dates, dry spells, livestock.

Then

Homework help, opening hours, general health information — never a diagnosis.

Private threads

Each phone number is a private thread.

Questions

Cost

Replies stay under 300 characters. One SMS is often cheaper than opening a data bundle for a single question.

Languages

Clear English first. Swahili, French, isiXhosa or isiZulu next. Plain text only. No markdown.

Privacy

Your number is your account. Each number stays a private thread. No signup, no password.
